# panorama-season3/ (Season 3 sale)

Season 3 sale, "Nothing Remains the Same" (Yigit Duman x Pale Kirill, https://x.com/palekirill), its own git repo (private GitHub `ygtdmn/panorama-season3`, branch main), built from a copy of `panorama-season-2-auction/` with the same layout (`contracts/` Foundry, `frontend/` Next.js on dev port 5476, e2e next 5477 + anvil 8547). Sells 30 tokens #181-#210 (six zones of five days: Interface, City, Suburb, Village, Nature, Abstract; each day has one of Pale Kirill's 30 themes, five per zone in the order figure / place / event / object / feeling, creator on Day 1 to love on Day 30, `themes` + `themeForToken` in `frontend/src/lib/season3Zones.ts`; the route board is his table, and the standings list days by theme grouped and coloured by zone). Bidding opens 2026-10-19 17:00 UTC for 24h (+ max 24h extension), reserve 0.1 ETH, increment 5%; Day 1 publishes 2026-10-20. `PanoramaSeason3Auction` = the S2 contract with these changes, deliberately not re-audited: `MAX_UNITS = 30` / `FIRST_TOKEN_ID = 181` (needs `totalMinted == 180`, mainnet's state after S2); `placeBid(string signal)` (optional ≤280-byte message for the day the bid wins, forwarded in the same transaction to the immutable `signalController`, never affects rank; `increaseBid` takes none); `MAX_BIDS_PER_WALLET = 2`; `BID_TICK = 0.001 ether` (every bid and top-up a whole number of ticks, else `AmountNotOnTick`; the reserve must be on the tick; the min increment and the extension threshold round up to the tick); and unique amounts once full (`bidsAtAmount[uint96]` counts active bids per amount while Active; once all 30 are held a new bid or a raised total equal to a held amount reverts `AmountTaken`; ties stay possible before the board fills and keep the earlier-bid tie-break). `minimumBid()` returns a placeable amount (rounded, stepped past held amounts); `isAmountAvailable(uint256)` is a helper view. `SPLIT_A_BPS = 5800` and the 7-arg constructor are unchanged from S2. Settlement opens the controller's season-3 cap to the exact winner count (`SettleAuctionAtomic`, one batch of 30). Frontend has its own S3 design in two layers. Panorama's layer is the structure: the PANORAMA / SEASON 3 mono wordmark (hero lockup, footer; there is no top bar on any page: "Enter Panorama ↗" is a button in the hero's top corner, recovery is linked from the FAQ, and /admin and /recovery open with a back link to the auction), Hanken Grotesk text and figures, JetBrains Mono uppercase labels (also `.game-caption` on images; the bid console uses IBM Plex Mono instead, by re-pointing `--font-mono` on `.sale-console`), the one-continuous-artwork story and the seven `--mood-*` colours. Pale Kirill's old-PC-game layer (geniusloci.palekirill.xyz plus the studio generations; not HUD/VHS) is the world: dark only (`:root` and `.dark` share tokens; `ThemeProvider` is a no-op), a black surround with framed game screens, translucent night-blue `.game-panel` windows, `.game-titlebar`, sunk `.game-slot` inventory cells, bevelled `.game-btn`, bold italic Arial `.game-menu` words (menu, zone names, section heads) and the season title in red Jacquard 24 blackletter (`.game-logo`). The hero (`HeroPanorama.tsx` → `HeroScreen`) is a title screen: the Season 3 tableaus published so far (`lib/season3Tableaus.ts`; the root `app/page.tsx` is a server component that reads the CDN `metadata.json` (ISR, 5 min) and hands the list to the client page `app/AuctionPage.tsx`, so the image URLs are in the first HTML; tableaus go through Next's image optimizer (`heroImage`: 1200w / 1920w webp, about 290 KB against 420-500 KB, cached by the optimizer since the CDN serves them no-cache), studio frames stay direct; `app/hooks/useSeason3Tableaus.ts` then re-reads the index every 10 min; the join between the newest tableau and the first studio frame can be hand-stitched on Comfy Cloud with the production seam workflow and listed in `HERO_JOINS`, keyed by the newest day's date (2026-10-22: #183 into frame 08, 512px band, no prompt, at `season3/hero/2026-10-22-v2/{183,08}.webp` (the first folder holds Cloudflare-cached 404s from before the upload), immutable), so it applies only while that day is the newest and the plain cut returns with the next reveal; tokens #181 on by position, each day as the next day joined it (`optimized_updated_<date>.webp`) and the newest as revealed, so consecutive days join as one panorama; the screen always holds eight frames (`HERO_FRAMES`): the newest published tableaus, then studio frames from 08 on to fill, so each new day replaces one studio frame until all eight are tableaus, after which it shows the eight newest; until the first read the screen stays dark, and only an unreadable index falls back to the whole studio strip) travel behind the PANORAMA / SEASON 3 lockup and the title; the studio strip, the 21 tiles of studio panorama `eJe7BeJ_W_XM` (1600x900 webp on the CDN at `season3/strip/2026-10-13/00..20.webp`, immutable cache; exported from the ops server's studio DB and uploaded with the production main-workflow's R2 credentials, since the local `.env` has none; a refresh goes to a new dated folder and `STRIP_BASE` in `HeroPanorama.tsx`; nothing is served from Next.js; it also supplies the zone tiles and section backgrounds) opens at frame 7.75 (the floating house) in that fallback, the tableaus at day 1; `useSteerableDrift` drifts the looped strip at 22px/s and hands control to drag (mouse/touch, momentum), sideways wheel/trackpad and arrow keys, easing the drift back in 2.6s after the last input (vertical wheel and touch scroll stay with the page). The nav is a bevelled "bid" button plus Panorama `bracket-link`s. Genius Loci is Kirill's own project: borrow its era, never its signature pieces (no play button, no stacked italic menu on the title screen). Right after the hero, `ArtistStatement.tsx` ("the statement", `#statement`, first in the hero menu): Pale Kirill's season statement verbatim, set as printed wall text in Newsreader (`font-statement`, used only here): no title line (removed at the user's request), body at reading size, an italic closing line, signed in small caps and linked to his X, over frame 20 under a calm veil; the hero intro is a provocation, not a summary ("You have lived in many homes. Some were houses. Some were people, cities, a single song. This one is a panorama, and every day a market decides how it feels.") Then `WorkChapters.tsx` ("the work"): Panorama's seasons as a chapter select (three cards whose pictures, S1 #70, the S2 header and studio frame 02, were joined edge to edge with the production seam workflow on Comfy Cloud (1024px band into S2, 1280px plus a stitch prompt into S3) and live at `season3/work/2026-10-13-v2/{1,2,3}.webp` on the CDN, so the row reads as one strip across the gaps; S2 is "Anthology of Mankind", S3 highlighted; the site stays Season 3 focused, so no cards for later days) and the seven-mood ladder. Studio tiles 0-5 are the six zones in order and head each zone on the **route board** (`RouteBoard.tsx`, the route and Kirill's themes merged into one section: rank r holds day r; at xl a grid of six zone rows, each headed by its tile, name and description, by his five kinds as columns (figure / place / event / object / feeling), every cell a day with its number, token, theme as the headline and the bid / holder (ENS name resolved when the cell nears the viewport, as in the ledger; off in demo) / signal / open state; below xl one block per zone with each day's kind inline; `landingRank` lights the day a typed bid would take, mirrored as "Lands on #N / Day N, Zone" in the console) beside a sticky console with a title bar (`page.tsx`; the console says things by shape and interaction, not paragraphs: the 30-tick strip lifts and outlines the day a typed bid would take, figures come first with their explanations in hover/tap tooltips, the bid form is a "Bid" header over the amount field ("Your maximum", the min chip, then one line inside the field: the day the typed bid would take, as a standing that pulses "now" with a tooltip on how higher bids push it later, or the reason it can't land, or with nothing typed "at the minimum, day N / theme"), and the signal field is always open with its rules behind an info tooltip and the character counter inside the field; its maximize toggle, desktop only, gives the console the whole row at 60rem / 77.5rem on 3xl and hides the reading column, remembered per browser under `panorama-s3:rail-max` and applied before first paint by `lib/railMax.ts`'s inline script stamping `html[data-rail-max]`, with the layout in `globals.css` on `.sale-grid` / `.sale-main` / `.sale-console`), a collapsible ledger (`Standings`), then `SeasonDetails.tsx` (signals over tile 10, the sale over tile 14, FAQ accordion, footer). Signals live in **`PanoramaSeason3SignalController`** (`contracts/src/`, own Ownable contract, `constructor(nft)`, deployed first by `DeployAuction`, then the auction with it as its 8th constructor arg, then `setAuction` once). Signals are **write-once with no deadline**: never changed or cleared, but they can be added later at any time (the user: "it's not an instruction, it's a signal"; the artists may use or ignore any of them, e.g. on Day 1, which reveals while bidding is open). `bidSignal[bidId]` is set by the auction's forward from `placeBid` (`recordBidSignal`, auction-only) or later by the live bid's bidder (`setBidSignal`); `tokenSignal[tokenId]` by the current `ownerOf` holder of #181-#210 or a wallet the holder delegated to on delegate.xyz (`setTokenSignal`; the controller asks v2 `checkDelegateForERC721(sender, holder, nft, tokenId, 0)` at `DELEGATE_REGISTRY` `0x0000…b493`, then v1 `checkDelegateForToken(sender, holder, nft, tokenId)` at `DELEGATE_REGISTRY_V1` `0x0000…638B`, both the same on mainnet and Sepolia, so a wallet, contract or single-token delegation counts (full rights in v2; v1 has none); no registry code or a failed call reads as no delegation, and one broken registry never hides the other; `DeployAuction` checks both addresses; `TokenSignalSet` names the holder; unit tests use mock registries etched at those addresses, `test/fork/DelegateRegistryFork.t.sol` runs against the real ones when `MAINNET_FORK_RPC` is set); every write checks non-empty (`EmptySignal`), <= 280 bytes (`SignalTooLong`) and an empty slot (`SignalAlreadySet`). A day's signal = the winning bid's if non-empty, else the holder's (`signalOf(tokenId, winningBidId)`, bid id from the auction's `Won`); a holder signal on a day whose bid already had one is stored but never becomes the day's. Frontend: `useSignalController.ts` reads controller storage by multicall (addresses from the auction's `signalController()` / `nft()`); the UI only ever offers "add a signal" (bid form field labelled optional, add it now or later; `SignalEditor.tsx` on "Your bids" and on "Your days" when the day's signal is empty) and every signal goes through an inline "This is permanent. Send it as written?" confirm; route / standings markers use the day signal (`lib/signalBoard.ts`); `/admin` Signals lists one row per day with holder, signal and source plus copy / JSON export, which is how the artists read signals when making a day. **After the sale** (`s.phase === "settled"`; the bidding UI stays for every earlier phase, so the e2e suite still covers it): the auction settled with 22 winners and the eight days it left open (#203-#210) are each on a reserve auction on Manifold's marketplace (`0x3a35…d799`; 0.1 ETH to open, 24h from the first bid, 5% raises, a bid in the last 10 minutes resets the clock to 10; tokens escrowed in the marketplace). `lib/dayAuctions.ts` maps each token to its listing id and Manifold page (mainnet only; #209's first listing 21190 was cancelled, the live one is 21192) and holds the payload types and `dayAuctionStatus` (open / live / ended / sold / withdrawn); `lib/dayAuctions.server.ts` reads every listing in one multicall (`getListing`, ABI subset `lib/abis/manifoldMarketplace.ts`), the bid history from `BidEvent` logs scanned from block 26,243,600 then only new blocks (`DAY_AUCTIONS_LOG_CHUNK`), block times and ENS, cached per process, through the server-only `RPC_URL` alone (no public fallback: public nodes refuse an `eth_getLogs` that far back; unset = 503 with the reason logged); the listings carry the response, while history, block times and names are best effort, so a failed log read still serves the sale; `GET /api/day-auctions` serves it (501 off mainnet, `s-maxage=10`), and `app/hooks/useDayAuctions.ts` is one shared 15s poll. The home page then: a short Panorama description and "N of 30 sold / M open for bids" above the statement; the hero's bid button goes to `/auctions`; the console (titlebar "the days") shows "N of 30 sold" (a day sold on Manifold counts and lights its tick), the final price, and `OpenDaysPanel` (`auction/components/OpenDays.tsx`: one row per day still taking bids, linking to Manifold; a day sold on Manifold leaves the list, and the panel goes once none remain) in place of the bid form; the route board's and the ledger's open days read "open for bids", a live clock, or sold, and link to their auction; the signals copy speaks to holders, "the sale" steps are gone and the FAQ keeps only what a visitor or holder needs (incl. "How can I get a day?"). `/auctions` ("Open for bids.") is the card view on one desktop screen: a back link, the title in red blackletter, one line on Panorama with the sold / open count, and a grid of cards for the days still taking bids only (4x2, one row when four or fewer, "Every day is sold." once none remain; zone tile, day and zone, theme, price and bidder, a live clock, earlier bids, a "bid on manifold" button), each a link to its Manifold page, where all bidding happens. Site origin `season3.panorama.garden`; the share card is a 1200x630 JPEG on the CDN (`season3/og/2026-10-13/season3-og.jpg`: the title screen at the floating house with the PANORAMA / SEASON 3 lockup, the red blackletter title, the two artists and the auction date; `OG_IMAGE_URL` + `OG_IMAGE_ALT` in `lib/constants.ts`, used by the root layout's Open Graph and Twitter tags), immutable, so a new card goes to a new dated folder.
